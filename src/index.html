The Technology Behind the Guard

01

Electrical Isolation Architecture

The guard's patented geometry and insulating material composition controls the transmission of electrical current from therapy devices to conductive dental materials — implants, fillings, crowns, and orthodontic hardware.

02

Medical-Grade Insulating Materials

Fabricated from electrically insulating materials including medical-grade silicone, EVA, and medical-grade rubber — selected for low conductivity, flexibility, biocompatibility, and autoclave sterilizability.

03

Universal Arch Geometry

The arch profile accommodates adult dentition without custom molding — a key patent claim. The flexible front wall deforms to fit different jaw sizes while maintaining consistent electrical isolation across the anterior teeth.

04

Variable Thickness Design

Thickness is engineered by zone: the anterior wall (over incisors) is thicker for maximum isolation, tapering toward posterior regions. This controlled geometry is a core patent claim — allowing isolation strength to be tuned by area.
