The Technology Behind the Guard

01

Dual-Layer Occlusal Architecture

The patented two-layer design separates the shock-absorbing core from the outer aesthetic shell — allowing the guard to flex under impact without transmitting force to the temporomandibular joint.

02

FDA-Compliant EVA Compound

Formulated from medical-grade ethylene-vinyl acetate with no BPA, phthalates, or latex. The compound is autoclave-compatible, odor-resistant, and maintains dimensional stability through repeated sterilization cycles.

03

Universal Arch Geometry

The arch profile is derived from population-averaged dental measurements, providing a secure non-custom fit across adult dentition without the cost or time of custom molding — a core claim in the patent filing.

04

Structural Color Integrity

The outer shell accepts pigment and brand colors at the molecular level during the molding process — not applied as a surface coating — so branding does not delaminate, fade, or compromise bite strength.
